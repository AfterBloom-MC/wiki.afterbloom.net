## What is AfterBloom?

AfterBloom is a Minecraft network focused on the players. We always prioritise your voice over our opinions. 

## What gamemodes do we have?

###[Survival](servers/Survival)
Survival is our take on the smp, it features an economy, custom worlds, player shops and much more!

## I'm having a problem! What do I do?

If you're having a problem on the network you can try the following:
> You could visit our [troubleshooting page](misc/troubleshooting), and search for your issue.  
> You could ask in our [discord](https://discord.gg/afterbloom)'s [#Support channel](https://discord.com/channels/1287562430448271430/1287765356240441427)  
> You could ask an ai of your choosing (this is a great one!)  
> You could also create a bug report in our [discord](https://discord.gg/afterbloom) by using /bug in any channel, and following instructions
